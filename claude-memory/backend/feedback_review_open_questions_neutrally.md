When Felix drafts a question for the team (e.g. in /write-check), review whether the question is clear and fairly framed. Don't flag it as wrong because it doesn't assume the option I recommended in our chat.

**Why:** In the group-auth team note (2026-10-13), I flagged his default-group paragraph as "describes a rule we didn't pick". But how to handle the default group was exactly the open decision he was taking to the team. He said the feedback "jumps two steps ahead".

**How to apply:** Separate decisions that are already locked from ones still pending. For pending ones, give feedback only on clarity and on whether each option and its consequences are stated correctly. Never treat my recommendation as the baseline the text has to match.
